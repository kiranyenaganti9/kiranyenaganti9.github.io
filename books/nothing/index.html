A novel by Kiran Yenaganti

Nothing

A novel about the hours we keep postponing.

A nurse caught between earning a living and being home. A retired cleaner who has spent forty years looking at a picture of the sea. A wealthy man who cannot buy back an evening.

When a research institute begins measuring the time people spend on things they never chose, three lives reveal what the ledger misses.

Nothing follows the cost of making room for a life, rather than waiting for the right time to begin one.
