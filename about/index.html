He/him · San Francisco Bay Area

I research and build runtime governance for autonomous AI agents: how an agent acts, when a person must approve, and what evidence supports the result. I founded DataGovernAI LLC in 2025 to do that work and to build the prototype that tests it.

How I got here

I left India in January 2019 at 22. It was my first flight. I wanted to build something of my own, and the first attempt was Techworkify, a technology staffing business. COVID and travel restrictions ended that plan before it started.

I went back to school — an MSc in Entrepreneurship & Innovation at Hult International Business School in San Francisco — and in 2025 founded DataGovernAI LLC.

The governance question came from a leasing assistant. Looking up availability and scheduling a tour sounded simple. But what if the record was thirty minutes old? What if the agent needed approval before making a commitment, and the thing it had been approved to do had changed by the time it acted? Those practical questions became a patent application, two manuscripts, and the research prototype.

The questions I work on

  • Runtime governance — enforcing policy at execution time, not only at design time.
  • Evidence validation — treating the facts an action depends on as something that ages and must be re-checked.
  • Human oversight — routing high-risk or binding actions to a person, with the reason attached.
  • Auditability and recovery — logging every decision, and giving failures an honest state instead of a blind retry.
